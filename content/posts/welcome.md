# Welcome to My Note-space

This is a space where I share my notes, thoughts, and learnings. Unlike traditional blogs with polished, finished articles, a digital garden embraces notes in various stages of completion. Some notes might be well-developed ideas, while others are seedlings — thoughts I'm still exploring and nurturing.

## What You'll Find Here

This site is a semi-refined reflection of my Obsidian vault, where I take notes on various topics I'm interested in or learning about. Here are some of the areas I focus on:

- **Cybersecurity and Tech trends before everything**
- **Learning Notes**: Selected concepts and ideas I'm studying.
- **Personal Reflections**: Thoughts on various subjects

## How to Navigate

You can browse content in several ways:

1. **Browse by date**: The home page shows the most recent notes
2. **Browse by tags or categories from the left pane**
3. **Search**: Use the search function to find specific topics

## About This Site

This site is built with **HTML, CSS & JS** and hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com). All content is written in Markdown within [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) and synchronized to this site via Git.

The beauty of this setup is that I can write notes in my preferred tool (Obsidian) with all its features for personal knowledge management, and easily share selected notes publicly through this site.
